### Price Elasticity Insights

**Price Elasticity Formula:** `Demand Change = 1 + (Elasticity × Price Change % × Sensitivity)`

- **Elastic segments** (Freelancers, Solopreneurs, Developers): −1.5 to −0.8
  - 10% price increase → 8-15% demand decrease
  - **Strategy:** Offer Micro tier ($12) as entry point to capture price-sensitive users

- **Moderately Elastic** (Micro Teams, Small Businesses): −1.2 to −0.6
  - 10% price increase → 6-12% demand decrease
  - **Strategy:** Team tier ($29/user) positioned as value play with collaboration features

- **Inelastic** (Mid-Market, Enterprise, Security): −0.8 to −0.3
  - 10% price increase → 3-8% demand decrease
  - **Strategy:** Premium tiers ($45-$65/user) with advanced features justify higher prices

## Comparative Analysis: 3-Tier vs. 5-Tier

### Should You Adopt 5 Tiers or Simplify to 3?

| Factor | 3-Tier Model | 5-Tier Model | Recommendation |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Simplicity** | ✓ Easier to explain | More complex decision | 3-tier wins for SMB focus |
| **Revenue Capture** | Leaves money on table | Captures more WTP variance | 5-tier wins for diverse segments |
