## 🎯 Recommended Pricing Strategy

### **BASELINE MULTI-TIER MODEL** (Highest LTV/CAC: 75.14x)

#### Tier Structure

1. **Individual Plan: $19/month**
   - Target: Freelancers, consultants, personal use
   - Features: Basic secret sharing, custom domain, single user
   - Positioning: Entry-level, competitive with market

2. **Team Plan: $29/user/month** ⭐ *Revenue Driver*
   - Target: Agencies, startups, SMBs (5-20 person teams)
   - Average team size: 5 users = **$145/month**
   - Features: Multiple accounts, extended retention, team management
   - Positioning: Sweet spot for SMB customers

3. **Enterprise Plan: $49/user/month**
   - Target: Regulated industries, large organizations
   - Average team size: 25 users = **$1,225/month**
   - Features: SSO, admin controls, compliance features, SLA
   - Positioning: Premium tier with advanced governance

#### Why This Model Wins

- **Highest Net Revenue Retention:** 110.6% (expansion revenue built-in)
- **Balanced Revenue Distribution:** No single tier exceeds 60% of revenue (43.4% max)
- **Market-Aligned Pricing:** $19-$49 range matches competitor positioning
- **Strong Customer Economics:** 3.2-month payback period enables aggressive growth

## 🏆 Competitive Benchmarking

### Market Positioning (Secret Management / Secure Sharing)

| Product | Individual | Team | Enterprise | Positioning |
|---------|-----------|------|------------|-------------|
| **OneTimeSecret** | **$19/mo** | **$29/user** | **$49/user** | Premium ephemeral secrets |
| 1Password | $8/mo | $20/user | Custom | Password management |
| Bitwarden | Free | $6/user | $12/user | Open-source, freemium |
| LastPass | $3/mo | $8/user | $12/user | Freemium password mgmt |
| Keeper | $35/yr | $45/user/yr | $60/user/yr | Enterprise security focus |
| Doppler | Free | $13/user | Custom | Developer secrets API |

### Differentiation Strategy

✓ **Ephemeral secrets** (vs. permanent password storage) — unique value prop
✓ **Custom domain + white-labeling** — appeals to agencies/consultancies
✓ **Zero-knowledge architecture** — strong privacy/security positioning
✓ **Focused feature set** — simplicity vs. bloated enterprise platforms

**Competitive Positioning:** OneTimeSecret Team tier at $29/user is positioned as a premium option compared to Bitwarden ($6) and LastPass ($8), justified by ephemeral secret use case and white-labeling capabilities.

### Medium-Term Optimizations (3-6 Months)

1. **Optimize Team Tier Adoption**
   - Current projection: 19.7% of customers
   - Target: 60%+ for maximum revenue impact
   - Tactics: Feature gating, team collaboration prompts, referral incentives

2. **Introduce Annual Plans with Discounts**
   - Test 15%, 20%, and 25% annual discounts
   - Benchmark: Most SaaS products offer 15-20% annual savings
   - Benefit: Improves cash flow and reduces churn

3. **Expand to Usage-Based Upsells**
   - Consider hybrid model: Base tier + overage charges
   - Example: "Team plan includes 1,000 secrets/month, $0.02/secret after"
   - Benefit: Captures expansion revenue from high-usage customers

### Long-Term Growth Strategies (12+ Months)

1. **Enterprise Sales Motion**
   - Hire enterprise sales rep when 10+ Enterprise customers

2. **Partner/Reseller Channel**
   - White-label offering for MSPs and consultancies
   - 20-30% partner margin on referrals
   - Target: 10-15% of revenue from partners by Year 3

3. **International Expansion**
   - Add multi-currency pricing (EUR, GBP)
   - Localize pricing pages for EU markets
   - Adjust pricing for purchasing power parity (PPP)
