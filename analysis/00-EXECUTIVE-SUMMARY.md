## 💡 Key Insights

### What We Learned

1. **Tiering Drives Transformational Growth**
   - 173% revenue increase possible in Year 2 from pricing restructure alone
   - Multi-tier model unlocks expansion revenue (194% NRR potential)
   - 10× value jumps between tiers align with customer journey

2. **Segment Optimization >Blanket Pricing**
   - S36 (Education) at -2.5 elasticity requires $9 pricing for optimal ARR
   - S05 (Tech Strategic) at -0.4 elasticity can support $299 (33× higher!)
   - One-size-fits-all pricing leaves $20M+ on table

3. **WTP is 2× More Important Than Any Other Variable**
   - Economic cycles drive 18.5% ARR swings (±10% WTP = ±$12M revenue)
   - Churn matters but has 4× lower impact than WTP
   - Implication: Macro monitoring > micro optimization

4. **High-Value Segments Have High Concentration Risk**
   - Strategic tier = 52% of revenue from 5% of customers
   - Enterprise concentration creates volatility in downturns
   - Diversification via Team tier growth essential (target: 35% of ARR)

5. **Competitive Differentiation Enables Premium Pricing**
   - 20-50% premium vs Comp A justified by features
   - 50-63% discount vs Comp B wins on value
   - Battle is won on value perception, not price alone

### What Surprised Us

- **Elastic segments are stable**: S36, S46 (high elasticity) have lower scenario volatility than S05, S10 (inelastic but WTP-sensitive)
- **Micro segments can be profitable**: S46 generates $3.2M ARR despite 22% churn (volume play)
- **Enterprise tier has lowest gross margin**: 76% vs 89% for Team tier (CSM costs)
