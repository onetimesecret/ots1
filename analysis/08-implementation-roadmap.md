## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)

### Objective
Establish pricing infrastructure, legal framework, and core team

### Initiatives

#### 1.1 Pricing Strategy Legal Review
- **Owner**: General Counsel
- **Team**: 1 FTE (external counsel)
- **Budget**: $80K
- **Deliverables**:
  - Antitrust compliance opinion
  - Segment-based pricing documentation
  - Contract template updates (all 5 tiers)
  - International pricing legal framework
- **Success Criteria**: Written legal approval to launch

#### 1.2 CPQ System Implementation
- **Owner**: Sales Operations Director
- **Team**: 2 FTEs (Salesforce consultant, Sales Ops Analyst)
- **Budget**: $250K ($200K software + $50K implementation)
- **Deliverables**:
  - Salesforce CPQ configured for 50 segments × 5 tiers
  - Approval workflows (discounts >10%)
  - Automated quoting (eliminate manual errors)
  - Integration with billing system
- **Success Criteria**: 100% of quotes generated via CPQ

#### 1.3 Pricing Committee Formation
- **Owner**: CFO
- **Team**: 0.5 FTE each from Finance, Product, Sales, Marketing
- **Budget**: $50K (internal time allocation)
- **Deliverables**:
  - Charter + decision-making framework
  - Quarterly review calendar
  - Price change approval process
  - Escalation protocols
- **Success Criteria**: First pricing decision made within 30 days

#### 1.4 Data Infrastructure
- **Owner**: Head of Data/Analytics
- **Team**: 2 FTEs (Data Engineer, Analyst)
- **Budget**: $200K ($100K hiring, $50K tools, $50K Clearbit/ZoomInfo)
- **Deliverables**:
  - Customer segmentation database
  - Real-time elasticity tracking
  - Competitive pricing monitoring
  - Dashboard infrastructure (Looker/Tableau)
- **Success Criteria**: 95% segment classification accuracy

### Phase 1 Totals
- **Budget**: $580K
- **Team**: 5.5 FTEs (2 new hires, 3.5 reallocated)
- **Timeline**: 90 days

## Risk-Adjusted Scenarios

### Downside Case (50% probability weight)

**Assumptions**:
- Slower customer acquisition (70% of plan)
- Higher churn (12% vs 10% target)
- Lower average ARPU (85% of plan)

**Outcomes**:
- Month 12 ARR: $35M (vs $50M base)
- Month 24 ARR: $65M (vs $95M base)
- Additional funding needed: +$3M

### Upside Case (20% probability weight)

**Assumptions**:
- Faster adoption (130% of plan)
- Enterprise tier over-performs
- International expansion accelerates

**Outcomes**:
- Month 12 ARR: $65M (vs $50M base)
- Month 24 ARR: $125M (vs $95M base)
- Path to profitability: Month 20 (vs Month 28)

### Blended Expected Value

**Expected ARR (Month 24)**:
- (0.30 × $65M downside) + (0.50 × $95M base) + (0.20 × $125M upside) = **$92M**

**Expected Return on Investment**:
- $92M ARR / $16M invested = 5.75× ROI

## Implementation Governance

### Steering Committee

**Members**:
- CEO (Chair)
- CFO
- CTO
- VP Sales
- VP Marketing
- Head of Customer Success

**Meeting Cadence**: Bi-weekly (Months 1-12), Monthly (Months 13-24)

**Responsibilities**:
- Review KPIs vs plan
- Approve budget reallocations (>$50K)
- Escalate blockers
- Adjust roadmap based on results

### Key Decision Points

**Month 3 (End of Phase 1)**: GO/NO-GO for Phase 2 pilot
- **Criteria**: CPQ operational, legal approval obtained, segments defined
- **If NO-GO**: Extend Phase 1, reallocate budget

**Month 6 (End of Phase 2)**: GO/NO-GO for full rollout
- **Criteria**: Pilot shows >20% ARR lift, churn <12%, positive customer feedback
- **If NO-GO**: Refine pricing, extend pilot

**Month 12 (End of Phase 3)**: GO/NO-GO for Phase 4 scale
- **Criteria**: $40M+ ARR achieved, NRR >100%, LTV:CAC >5×
- **If NO-GO**: Focus on efficiency vs growth

## Success Criteria & Exit Triggers

### Green Lights (Proceed as Planned)

- ARR growth >15% quarter-over-quarter
- Churn <10% annually
- LTV:CAC >5×
- Gross margin >80%
- NRR >100%
- Win rate vs competitors >55%

### Yellow Lights (Course Correct)

- ARR growth 10-15% QoQ
- Churn 10-15% annually
- LTV:CAC 3-5×
- NRR 90-100%
- Win rate 45-55%

**Actions**: Pricing optimization, churn task force, marketing reallocation

### Red Lights (Pivot or Pause)

- ARR growth <10% QoQ
- Churn >15% annually
- LTV:CAC <3×
- NRR <90%
- Win rate <45%

**Actions**: Freeze hiring, revisit pricing strategy, consider Tier/segment elimination
