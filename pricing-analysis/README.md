## Executive Summary

This comprehensive pricing analysis provides data-driven recommendations for OneTimeSecret's pricing structure based on:
- Market research of **50+ comparable SaaS products**
- **10,000 Monte Carlo simulations** per pricing scenario
- Statistical validation with 95% confidence intervals
- Detailed migration strategy for 230 existing customers

**Recommendation**: Implement **7-tier pricing structure** with intermediate tiers at $49, $125, and $300 to fill market gaps and maximize revenue capture.

**Expected Impact**: **+60% MRR growth** ($8,050 → $12,900) with 92% customer retention.
