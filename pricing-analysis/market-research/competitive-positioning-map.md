## Positioning Statement

**For**: Development teams and security-conscious organizations
**Who**: Need to share secrets securely with one-time access guarantees
**OneTimeSecret**: Is an ephemeral secret sharing platform
**That**: Provides enterprise-grade security with developer-friendly pricing

**Unlike**: Traditional password managers (1Password, LastPass) that focus on permanent storage
**And unlike**: Infrastructure tools (HashiCorp Vault) that require complex setup
**OneTimeSecret**: Offers instant deployment, one-time burn guarantees, and smooth pricing gradients

**We provide**: The security of HashiCorp Vault, the ease of 1Password, at a price competitive with Bitwarden.

## Win Themes by Competitor

### vs. 1Password / LastPass (Permanent Password Managers)
**Win Theme**: "Ephemeral security for one-time secrets"
- Their strength: Consumer brand, permanent password storage
- Their weakness: Not designed for ephemeral secrets, no one-time burn
- Our advantage: Purpose-built for temporary secret sharing

### vs. HashiCorp Vault (Infrastructure Secret Management)
**Win Theme**: "Enterprise security without infrastructure complexity"
- Their strength: Infrastructure-grade, full featured
- Their weakness: Complex setup, expensive ($360+), requires devops expertise
- Our advantage: 75% cheaper entry, instant deployment, same security standards

### vs. Bitwarden (Open Source Password Manager)
**Win Theme**: "Enterprise features at competitive pricing"
- Their strength: Open source, very affordable ($4-6)
- Their weakness: Limited enterprise features (no SSO at lower tiers)
- Our advantage: SSO at $125, compliance at $125, better gradients

### vs. Auth0 / Okta (Identity Platforms)
**Win Theme**: "Focused secret sharing without identity overhead"
- Their strength: Full identity platform, established enterprise
- Their weakness: Massive pricing gaps (Auth0 B2C $35 → $240 jump)
- Our advantage: Smooth gradients, focused use case, 40-50% lower cost

### vs. Postman / GitHub (Developer Platforms)
**Win Theme**: "Specialized secret security for developer workflows"
- Their strength: Broad developer platform, large user base
- Their weakness: Not specialized for secrets, pricing gaps (GitHub $4 → $21)
- Our advantage: Secret-specific features, smoother pricing, better security
