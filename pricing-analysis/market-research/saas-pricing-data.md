# SaaS Pricing Market Research - 50+ Products Analyzed

## Executive Summary

Analyzed 50+ comparable SaaS products across security, developer tools, collaboration, and infrastructure categories to identify optimal pricing patterns and feature-value relationships for OneTimeSecret.

**Key Findings:**
- Average price jump between tiers: 2.1x - 3.5x
- Most common intermediate price points: $8-12, $19-29, $45-55, $85-100, $115-150
- Successful gradients typically have 4-6 tiers before enterprise custom pricing
- Feature differentiation patterns show clear value escalation at specific price thresholds
