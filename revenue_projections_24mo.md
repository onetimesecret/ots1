## 1. DETAILED ASSUMPTIONS & CALCULATION METHODOLOGY

### 1.1 Pricing Structure (Recap)

|------|--------------|----------------|--------------|
| Free | $0 | Individuals, trials | 10 secrets/mo, 24hr max TTL, community support |
| Starter | $15 | Solo users | 100 secrets/mo, 7-day max TTL, email support |
| Professional | $49 | Small business (1-10 users) | Unlimited secrets, 30-day TTL, API access, priority support |
| Business | $199 | Teams, custom domains | Unlimited, 90-day TTL, custom domain, SLA, dedicated support |

### 1.2 Migration Assumptions (Month 1)

**Grandfathering Strategy:**
- All 230 existing customers offered to stay at $35/month for 6 months (through Month 6)
- Acceptance rate: 95% (218 customers stay, 12 churn due to uncertainty)
- Month 7: Grandfathered customers migrate to new tiers

**Rationale:** Reduces migration shock, maintains revenue stability, builds goodwill

### 1.3 Customer Acquisition Assumptions

**New Customer Ramp (Monthly Additions):**

| Month | Free Tier | Starter | Professional | Business | Total New | Rationale |
|-------|-----------|---------|--------------|----------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | Migration month only |
| 2-3 | 30 | 10 | 5 | 1 | 46 | Soft launch, existing channels |
| 4-6 | 50 | 20 | 10 | 3 | 83 | Marketing ramp-up |
| 7-12 | 60 | 25 | 12 | 4 | 101 | Steady state growth |
| 13-18 | 70 | 30 | 15 | 5 | 120 | Market penetration increasing |
| 19-24 | 80 | 35 | 18 | 6 | 139 | Mature growth phase |

**Assumptions:**
- Free tier grows 40% faster than current state (accessible to more users)
- Starter tier captures individual users previously priced out at $35
- Professional tier grows at similar rate to current business
- Business tier grows slower (premium positioning, niche market)

**Source:** Based on competitor benchmarks:
- Yopass (managed): ~$9/mo captured 100+ customers in first year (per Elest.io)
- PasswordPusher: 40M+ downloads suggests large free user base
- NordPass Teams: $1.79 tier grows 3x faster than Business tier

**Confidence:** Medium (65%) - Requires marketing investment to achieve

### 1.4 Churn Rate Assumptions

**Monthly Churn by Tier:**
- Free: 15% (high volatility, low commitment)
- Starter: 8% (individual users, moderate stability)
- Professional: 4% (business use, good retention)
- Business: 2% (high value, strong lock-in)
- Grandfathered: 5% (legacy customers, mid-tier retention)

**Churn Reduction Factors:**
- Product improvements: -0.5% monthly churn reduction (all tiers)
- Customer success initiatives: -0.3% (Professional/Business only)
- Stabilizes at: Free 12%, Starter 6%, Pro 3%, Business 1.5% (Month 24)

### 1.5 Free-to-Paid Conversion

**Conversion Rate:** 5% monthly (industry benchmark)
**Conversion Distribution:**
- 60% → Starter ($15)
- 35% → Professional ($49)
- 5% → Business ($199)

**Source:** ProfitWell freemium conversion benchmarks, OpenView SaaS benchmarks

### Calculation Methodology

**Customer Growth:**
```
New_Customers[month] = Base_Acquisition_Rate × Growth_Factor[month]
End_Customers[month] = Start + New - Churned + Free_Conversions
```

**Churn Calculations:**
```
Churned[tier, month] = Start_Customers[tier] × Churn_Rate[tier, month]
Churn_Rate[tier, month] = Base_Churn[tier] - Improvement_Factor[month]
```

**MRR Calculations:**
